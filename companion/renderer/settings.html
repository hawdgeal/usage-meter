Where each card sits (also the button on each card)

Floating: a window of its own, anywhere. On top of its app: just above Claude's or ChatGPT's window, hidden while the app is minimized. In its app's sidebar: laid into the sidebar, as wide as it. In Claude's session rail: the same, in the panel on the right of Claude's window.

Drag a card by any part that isn't a button; right-click it for more.

Extension not found?

Chrome, Edge and other Chromium browsers only let listed extensions talk to this app. Installed copies are found automatically. If yours isn't, copy its ID from the browser's extensions page (turn on Developer mode) and add it here. Firefox needs no ID.
